Strategy & process

A four-stage GTM process, from market data to a validated narrative.

01

Market & competitive research

Synthesized company and market data (Crunchbase, Sensor Tower, analyst reports) and benchmarked BeReal against Snapchat and TikTok on the retention mechanics driving each platform's MAUs.

02

User insight synthesis

Coded sentiment themes across both platforms using manual and AI-assisted analysis, cross-validating findings to isolate the single highest-leverage pain point worth solving for.

03

Positioning & concept design

Reframed BeReal's streak mechanic around its authenticity pillars, deliberately positioning it against the gamified patterns used by Snapchat and TikTok rather than copying them.

04

Validation & GTM narrative

Ran a cross-platform focus group and survey, then built an evidence-backed pitch translating research into a business case, closing with a clear recommendation for stakeholders.

Positioning & messaging

A messaging framework built to hold up against gamified competitors.

"Constellation Streaks: the only streak that forgives you."

Authentic

No badges, no rewards, no achievement pop-ups, a deliberate contrast to Snapchat and TikTok's gamified retention loops.

Forgiving

A missed day fades into the pattern instead of resetting to zero, directly answering BeReal's top-cited user complaint.

Distinct

Built on BeReal's existing habit loop rather than importing a mechanic from a competitor, so it reads as native to the brand.
